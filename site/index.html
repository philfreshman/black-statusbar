Why Nightbar

The menu bar you want, without giving up your wallpaper.

On a bright wallpaper macOS turns the menu bar light and the icons dark. Nightbar keeps it black, so the notch blends in and your icons stay white.

Any wallpaper

Bright photos, rotating folders and live wallpapers all get the same black bar.

Every display

Each connected screen gets its own black strip, and it follows when you plug displays in or out.

Hands off your wallpaper

Your wallpaper files are never edited. Turn Nightbar off and everything is exactly as before.

Launch at login

Switch it on once from the Nightbar menu and forget about it.

One switch

A single menu with an on and off toggle. No settings window, no accounts, no tracking.

Open source

A few hundred lines of Swift and AppKit with no dependencies. Read it, build it, improve it.

How it works

A black strip, just above your wallpaper.

macOS does not let apps paint the menu bar. It picks the bar's look and the icon colour from whatever sits behind it. So Nightbar puts black there.

  • 1Your wallpaper stays where it is, untouched.
  • 2Nightbar's strip sits right above it, behind the menu bar of every screen.
  • 3Desktop icons and windows stay on top, so nothing else changes.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Get Nightbar.zip from the latest release and unzip it.

Move it

Drag Nightbar.app into your Applications folder.

Open it

The first time, macOS asks you to confirm. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Keep it

Click the Nightbar icon in the menu bar and turn on Launch at Login.

Nightbar is not notarized by Apple yet, which is why macOS asks before the first launch. If you prefer the terminal, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nightbar.app once. Or build it yourself from source with ./scripts/build-app.sh.

FAQ

Questions

Does Nightbar change my wallpaper?

No. It never touches your wallpaper files or settings. It only draws a black strip behind the menu bar, which disappears when you turn Nightbar off or quit it.

Does it work with live and dynamic wallpapers?

Yes. Because the strip sits on top of the wallpaper, it does not matter what the wallpaper is doing underneath.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Releases are signed ad hoc rather than with an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper asks you to confirm once. The full source is on GitHub if you want to check it or build it yourself.

How do I turn it off or remove it?

Click the Nightbar icon in the menu bar and toggle Nightbar off, or choose Quit. To uninstall, turn off Launch at Login, quit, and move the app to the Bin.

Can I contribute?

Yes, please. Read the contributing guide, then open an issue or a pull request.
